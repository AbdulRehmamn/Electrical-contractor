Safety First

Signs Your Electrical Panel May Need Attention

Common red flags of a failing, hazardous, or obsolete electrical panel include:

Frequent circuit breaker tripping under standard appliance loads
Crackling, buzzing, or sizzling sounds emanating from the breaker enclosure
Discoloration, scorch marks, or persistent acrid burning smells
Panel enclosure is warm or hot to the touch during operation
Lights flickering or dimming whenever the air conditioning cycles on
Rust, oxidation, or moisture condensation inside the steel box
Panel brand is Federal Pacific (FPE) Stab-Lok, Zinsco, or Sylvania

Modern Electrical Infrastructure

Should I Upgrade to a 200-Amp Panel?

Homes built before 1990 were frequently wired with 100-amp or 125-amp service panels. In modern California households, high-draw electric vehicles, induction cooktops, heat pumps, and air conditioning quickly overload these legacy panels.

Upgrading to a 200-amp service provides ample electrical overhead, prevents repeated circuit trips, enhances fire safety, and elevates your property's resale value.

What are the key benefits of upgrading?
A 200-amp panel provides the electrical capacity required for Level 2 EV charging, central HVAC heat pump systems, modern solar and battery storage setups, and dedicated high-draw appliance circuits.
How long does the installation take?
Most main service panel upgrades are completed within a single working day (typically 6 to 8 hours), restoring your power before the evening so your household routine is minimally disrupted.
Do you pull city permits and handle inspections?
Yes. Duarte Electrical Services handles the permit application, blueprints, utility coordination with Southern California Edison, and city building inspector sign-off.
