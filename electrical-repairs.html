Diagnostic Warning Indicators

Signs Your Home Needs Immediate Electrical Repair

Do not ignore minor electrical anomalies. They frequently indicate deteriorating insulation or loose high-voltage splices:

Outlets that emit sparks when appliances are inserted or removed
Wall switch plates or electrical faceplates warm to the touch
Persistent burning, plastic, or fishy chemical smells near electrical boxes
GFCI reset buttons that fail to latch or trip immediately when reset
Buzzing, clicking, or hum noises coming from switches or recessed lighting cans

Troubleshooting Solutions

Common Electrical Repairs We Perform

From older mid-century homes in San Diego to modern custom residences in Newport Coast, our team has encountered and repaired every electrical challenge.

Circuit Breaker Troubleshooting & Replacement
When a breaker repeatedly trips, we perform resistance and load testing to determine whether the breaker is mechanically defective or if an active short circuit exists along the branch line.
Aluminum Wiring Remediation
Homes constructed between 1965 and 1973 often contain aluminum branch wiring. We install specialized COPALUM or AlumiConn connectors to safely terminate connections and eliminate fire risk.
Whole-Home Rewiring & Grounding
Older two-prong ungrounded circuits pose shock risks for sensitive electronics. We install modern three-wire Romex conductors and establish verified earth-ground electrode connections.
