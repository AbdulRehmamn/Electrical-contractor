Charging Speeds Explained

Select Your Ideal Charging Setup

Level 1 Charging • Basic & Slow

Plugs directly into a standard 120-volt household receptacle. Delivers only 3 to 5 miles of range per charging hour. Often requires 24+ hours for a full battery replenish.

Level 2 Charging • Fast & Affordable

Operates on a dedicated 240-volt circuit (40 to 60 amps). Delivers 25 to 44 miles of driving range per hour. Your vehicle fully charges overnight while electricity rates are lowest. The gold standard for modern homeowners.

DC Fast Charging • Commercial Grade

Commercial high-voltage direct-current systems (480V) engineered for business parking structures, fleets, and public facilities. Charges compatible EVs to 80% capacity in 20 to 30 minutes.

Diagnostic Troubleshooting

Signs Your EV Charger May Need Repairs

Even robust charging hardware can experience electrical faults over time. Common symptoms include:

Charging session stops prematurely without vehicle battery being full
Circuit breaker trips immediately upon plugging the connector into the vehicle
Status LED indicator displays red error flashing sequence
Connector head, handle, or cable feels abnormally hot during charging
Charging speed has degraded substantially below standard kilowatt rating
