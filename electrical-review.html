Inspection Questions

Electrical Review Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to questions regarding real estate inspections, safety reviews, and code updates.

When should a property undergo an electrical review?
We recommend an electrical review before purchasing a home, prior to major remodeling, when adding high-draw loads (EV chargers, heat pumps, pools), or if a home is more than 30 years old.
How does this differ from a general home inspection?
General home inspectors only check basic outlet operation. Our licensed master electricians open panels, test breaker calibration, inspect grounding rods, check wire gauges, and perform voltage drop load tests.
Can you repair any issues discovered during the review?
Yes. Our service vans carry common breakers, GFCI receptacles, and wiring components so urgent safety issues can often be corrected immediately.
