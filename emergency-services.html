Hazard Alert Guide

Situations Requiring Immediate Emergency Electrical Dispatch

Never attempt to touch or repair high-voltage equipment experiencing active failures:

Active sparks, electrical arcing, or visible smoke from any panel or receptacle
Water intrusion, plumbing leaks, or flooding reaching the main electrical service box
Total electrical outage isolated only to your home while neighbor power is active
Main 200-amp service disconnect trips and will not latch when switched
Electric shocks or mild tingling sensations felt when touching appliances

Emergency Safety Steps

What to Do During an Electrical Emergency

Critical steps to safeguard your property while awaiting the arrival of our certified technician.

1. De-Energize Safely if Accessible
If you can safely reach the main breaker without standing in water or approaching arcing equipment, switch the main disconnect to OFF. If there is visible fire, evacuate and call 911 immediately.
2. Keep Clear of Water & Conductive Surfaces
Do not enter flooded basements, crawlspaces, or utility rooms where water may have contacted subpanels or outlets. Electrified water can be lethal.
3. Call Duarte Electrical Services at (619) 805-6267
Our live dispatcher will take your address, verify critical safety status, and route the nearest on-call master electrician immediately to your location.
