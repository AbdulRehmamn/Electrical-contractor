Duarte Electrical Services

Duarte Electrical Services is 24 years in the making (DES) and emerged in 2016 as an electrical contractor serving San Diego County. We are dedicated to never compromising quality and performance in today’s evolving technology. We play a comprehensive role in providing our clients with the ability to achieve their projects.

An Array of Specialized Solutions

We offer an array of solutions to meet specific needs, including energy efficiency and conservation, alternative energy resources, heavy loads and machinery, infrastructure builds incorporating utility connections, Industrial applications, Commercial applications, Residential applications, New construction, Tenant improvements, and Control wiring and methods. We operate from 600 volts AC/DC to 10 volts AC/DC.

We are committed to continually keeping up with the latest technology in the electrical field and educating ourselves on the latest electrical compliance and consumption demands.
