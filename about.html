Pristine Power • Service You Can Trust

For more than a decade, Pristine Power Electric has stood as a dependable beacon for property owners who demand certified technical precision. Our certified journeymen electricians handle installations, diagnostics, and repairs with meticulous craftsmanship.

Decades of Proven Experience
Our team brings extensive real-world expertise in electrical engineering, panel modernization, high-voltage vehicle charging infrastructure, and architectural lighting installations.
Honest Pricing & First-Visit Solutions
We believe in 100% transparent flat-rate quotes without hidden fees. Our service vehicles carry comprehensive equipment inventory to ensure the vast majority of challenges are resolved in a single visit.
24/7 Response When You Can't Wait
Electrical faults do not follow business hours. Our dedicated emergency crews are dispatched promptly day or night, ensuring critical safety hazards are neutralized fast.
Electrical Services for Every Need
From residential main panel replacements to commercial tenant improvements, code violation remedies, and smart SPAN panel setups, our certified team handles every scope of work.
